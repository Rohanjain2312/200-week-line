How we tested it

We wrote the question, the cutoffs and the rule for the answer down before looking at any result. A hash of that plan is saved in the project, so changes show up.

  1. Each week we found good companies in the S&P 500. Good means a high return on money invested, steady cash flow and low debt.
  2. We marked the ones whose price was within 5% of the 200-week average and had just come down to it.
  3. We compared what those earned over the next 1, 3 and 5 years with good companies far above the line that same week, after trading costs of 10 basis points each way.
  4. Years from 2016 on were locked away until the code was final, and opened once.

Where the data falls short

Free price data lacks many companies that were later dropped from the index. If too many are missing, the plan says the honest answer is "cannot tell". The page shows the same weight for a "cannot tell" as for a "helps".
