Does a stock near its 200-week average do better?

The 200-week average is the average price over the last 200 weeks. We asked whether good companies bought when their price sits close to that average beat similar companies bought the same week.

The data cannot tell.

The data cannot say whether buying quality companies near their 200-week average helps. Too many missing companies. The range of what the data cannot rule out runs from -6.3 to 2.6 points a year, so the data does not settle the question. The weekly list is shown for interest, and a forward scoreboard will test it as new years arrive.

Cannot tell does not mean no harm. In 6 of 6 test and holding-time pairs the best guess was below zero, and the ranges still include zero or a small gain. Random picks did better than the dip picks in most draws for 6 of 6 pairs.
