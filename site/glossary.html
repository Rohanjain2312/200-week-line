Words explained

200-week average
The average closing price over the last 200 weeks.
S&P 500
A list of about 500 large US companies.
Return on invested capital
Profit after tax divided by the money put into the business.
Free cash flow
Cash a company makes from its business after paying for equipment.
Net debt
Debt minus cash.
Points a year
A gap of 1 point a year means one percentage point more yearly return.
Range
The spread of results the data cannot rule out. If it holds zero, no gap is shown.
Survivor bias
Looking only at companies that still exist makes past results look better than they were.
Delisted
Removed from a stock exchange, for example after a buyout or failure.
Holdout
Years locked away until the plan was final, then opened once.
